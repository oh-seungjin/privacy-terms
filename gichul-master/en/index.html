EXAM · PRACTICE · RECORD

Practice past exam questions by year, then redo your mistakes

Solve past questions of Korean national license exams, such as the real estate agent exam, by year and subject, and repeat the ones you missed.

  • Only questions checked against the official final answer key
  • Wrong-answer notes, bookmarks, statistics, mock exams, resume where you stopped
  • Free sample questions in every subject; passes last 30 or 90 days and never auto-renew

Your study records stay on your device, with no ads and no tracking. You sign in only if you want to buy a pass. Questions and answers come from the Human Resources Development Service of Korea (Q-Net) under the Korea Open Government License Type 1. This is an independent app and is not affiliated with that organization. Content is in Korean.
