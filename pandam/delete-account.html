Delete account

Effective November 10, 2026

1. Delete in the app

Open Account, select Delete Pandam data, and confirm. This permanently removes the Pandam profile, matches, cloud records, push tokens, and unified data. Standalone game accounts remain separate unless the screen explicitly says otherwise.

2. Delete without app access

Email dhalska2@gmail.com with the subject “Pandam Data Deletion” and identify the Apple or Google sign-in address used for Pandam. We may request a safe ownership check.

3. What may remain

Apple and Google retain store transaction records under their own policies. We may retain minimal de-identified purchase or refund markers where required to prevent duplicate grants, fraud, or meet legal duties.
