Support

Effective November 10, 2026

1. Getting started

Choose a game from Home, then select live or correspondence play. My Games shows turns across all five games.

2. Account and synchronization

Use Apple on iOS or Google on Android to protect your Pandam identity. Existing anonymous game data is merged when supported. Sign out and back in with the same provider to restore access.

3. Purchases

Open Premium and choose Restore purchases if an entitlement is missing. Subscription cancellation and refunds are managed in App Store or Google Play settings.

4. Notifications and troubleshooting

Enable notifications in both Pandam and device settings. If a problem continues, email the device model, OS version, app version, and steps to reproduce it without including passwords or private game messages.
