Privacy

Effective November 10, 2026

1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to the Pandam iOS and Android app (com.osj.pandam), operated by Next Studio in South Korea. Contact: dhalska2@gmail.com.

2. Accounts and game data

Pandam creates an anonymous online identity so matches can work without sign-in. If you choose Apple or Google sign-in, we receive the provider account identifier and, when supplied, email address. We store profiles, ratings, settings, game records, live and correspondence matches, invitations, and account-merge records in Supabase so play can continue across devices.

3. Purchases, ads, and diagnostics

Apple and Google process payments. Pandam sends product and transaction or purchase-token proof to its server to verify subscriptions and permanent ad removal; it does not receive card details. Where ads are enabled, Google AdMob may process device information, approximate IP-based location, and an advertising identifier where the platform permits it. Firebase Crashlytics processes crash diagnostics, app/OS version, device model, stack traces, and an installation identifier in release builds.

4. Notifications and service providers

If you enable notifications, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token, language, time zone, and game notification settings are stored to deliver turn and match alerts. Supabase, Apple, Google Play, Firebase, and AdMob process data as service providers, and processing may occur outside your country.

5. Retention, security, and deletion

Data is encrypted in transit and retained while the account is active or as needed for security, purchase verification, refunds, and legal duties. In the app, open Account and choose Delete Pandam data. You may also use the deletion page or email us. Store transaction records controlled by Apple or Google follow their policies.

6. Children and changes

Pandam is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal data. Material policy changes will be posted here with a revised date.
