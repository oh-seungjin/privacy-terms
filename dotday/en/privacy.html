Dotday Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Dotday is an offline countdown app provided by Next Studio. It is designed to work without an account or server connection.

1. Information handled on your device

Countdown titles, dates, icons, colors, yearly-repeat settings and reminder schedules are stored only on your device. The home screen widget reads the nearest countdowns from storage shared on the same device.

2. Collection and sharing

Next Studio does not collect or transmit your countdowns to a server. The app has no account, cloud sync, advertising, analytics or tracking SDK, and it does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

3. Crash reporting

To fix crashes, Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymous diagnostics such as error logs, device model, operating-system version and app version. This does not include what you type, such as countdown titles, and is not used to identify you.

4. Notifications

If you grant permission, Dotday schedules local reminders on your device at 9 AM the day before and on the day. Reminder content and times are not sent to a server. You can revoke permission in device settings.

5. Deletion

You can delete each countdown in the app, and uninstalling the app removes the records from your device, subject to system backup and restore behavior.

6. Children and changes

Dotday is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated.
