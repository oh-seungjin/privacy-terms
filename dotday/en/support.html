Dotday Help & Support

Review these tips or email us if you need help using the app.

If reminders do not arrive

Tap the bell icon at the top right to request permission, and confirm notifications are allowed for Dotday in device settings. Reminders are scheduled on the device, not by a server.

If the widget looks out of date

Open the app once and the widget refreshes. It shows up to the three nearest countdowns.

Delete data

Open a countdown and tap Delete, or uninstall the app to remove records from the device.

When contacting us

Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue.
