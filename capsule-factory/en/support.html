Capsule Factory Help and Support

If something goes wrong, check below or email us.

1. How to play

Colored blocks roll down the conveyor. Tap a box in the pool to send it to the docks and the matching blocks get packed. If every dock fills up you are stuck, so think about the order.

2. Undo and revive

Each attempt has one free undo. When you are stuck you can optionally watch an ad to return to the last winnable position (once per attempt). If the ad cannot load, the revive is not used up and you can restart for free. A restart is a new attempt and is unlimited.

3. Your progress

Progress is saved automatically on your device and continues after you close the app. It is not moved to other devices (it may be restored through Android backup).

4. Privacy choices

In the EEA and other regions where it applies, change your ad-consent choice with the icon at the top right of the stage list. The Settings menu opens the privacy policy.

5. When you contact us

Please include your device model, Android version, app version and the steps that reproduce the problem.
