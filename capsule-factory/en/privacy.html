Capsule Factory Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Capsule Factory is a puzzle game provided by Next Studio. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no purchases, and the developer does not store any user data on a server. Google AdMob processes data to show ads.

1. Data stored on your device

Cleared stages, the level in progress, how many undos and revives you used and the animation speed setting are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are not sent to the developer. If Android auto backup is on, this file may be included in your Google account backup (Google Drive) and restored on a new device. Google manages that backup and you can turn it off in device settings.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device or other identifiers (advertising ID, app set ID), app interactions (ad impressions, taps, video views), diagnostics and performance information, the IP address and the approximate location derived from it, and device and OS information, and share it with Google and its advertising partners. Purposes: serving ads, measuring ad performance, and preventing fraud and abuse. The developer does not read or store this data and only sees aggregated statistics in the AdMob console. Data is sent encrypted. Details: https://policies.google.com/privacy , https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Consent (UMP)

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, some US states and other places where the law requires it, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) shows an ad-consent screen. There you can change your choice at any time with the 'Privacy choices' icon on the stage list. The UMP SDK stores your consent status on the device and the app does not request ads before consent allows it. You can keep playing without watching ads. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

4. Sharing

The developer does not sell your data. Ad-related data is processed by Google and its advertising partners as described above. No other third party receives data from the app.

5. Retention and deletion

The developer holds no user data on servers, so there is nothing to retain or delete there. Delete on-device data by uninstalling the app or using Settings > Apps > Capsule Factory > Storage > Clear data, and delete backups in your Google account settings. You can access or delete ad-related data at https://myaccount.google.com .

6. Children and changes

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If this policy changes, this page will show a new effective date.
