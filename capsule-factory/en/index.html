PUZZLE · CONVEYOR · BRAIN

Pick the right boxes for the blocks on the conveyor

Capsule Factory is a brain-teasing puzzle: colored blocks roll down a conveyor and you choose which box to send to the docks, and in what order.

  • A short tutorial, then puzzles that make you think
  • Every stage is verified to be solvable
  • One free undo and unlimited free restarts
  • Automatic saving: close the app and continue later

No account needed and it works offline; your progress stays on your device. The game contains ads: only optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch.
