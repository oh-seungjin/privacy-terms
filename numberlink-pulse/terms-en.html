1. Service and storage

NumberLink Pulse is a puzzle about clearing pairs with equal values or a sum of ten. Straight connections and row-boundary connections apply: empty cells may be crossed, other numbers may not. It offers generated stages, daily puzzles/missions, time trials and ranked play. Progress is saved locally and synchronized when online. Account operations, ad rewards, hint spending and purchase verification require an internet connection.
