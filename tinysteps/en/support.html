TinySteps Help & Support

Effective October 9, 2026

Review these tips or email us if you need help using the app.

1. If reminders do not arrive

Confirm that notification permission is enabled for TinySteps and that the habit's selected days and time match your schedule. Reminders are scheduled on the device, not by a server.

2. Data backup

Data Export copies JSON to the clipboard. TinySteps does not provide accounts or cloud sync.

3. Delete data

Use Reset All Data in Settings. This removes habits, focus history, garden state, and collection progress from the device.

4. When contacting us

Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue. You do not need to include private habit details.
