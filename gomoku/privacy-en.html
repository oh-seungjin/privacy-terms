Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

1. Who we are

Hansu Gomoku (the “Service”) is operated by Next Studio. Send privacy questions to the email address below.

2. Information we process

The Service uses an anonymous Supabase authentication ID by default. If you choose to recover your progress across devices, you can link an Apple or Google account, in which case the account identifier and email passed on by the sign-in provider may be processed. While you use the Service we process: your nickname, your avatar, and a country code taken from your device region setting (shown to opponents together with your avatar and rating as a flag); match moves and results, ratings, learning progress and game records, friends, blocks and reports, missions, achievements, coins, season and arena participation, avatar unlocks and daily check-in records, and learning content access records. If you turn on correspondence-game alerts: a push notification device token, the device type (Android or iOS), the device language and scheduled alert jobs. If the app crashes: the device and app version, the time of the error and a stack trace. For in-app purchases: the product ID, transaction ID and receipt verification result (we do not collect payment card details; Apple or Google processes payment).

3. Why we use it

Only to provide online play and matchmaking, calculate rankings and rewards, run friends and spectating, send alerts, prevent abuse, handle reports, verify and restore purchases, and analyze crashes and improve quality.

4. Service providers

Supabase: authentication, game data, server functions and real-time sync. Apple and Google: the sign-in you choose, in-app payments and purchase restore. Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering correspondence-game alerts. Google Firebase Crashlytics: crash analysis. Google AdMob: interstitial and rewarded ads and ad measurement. We do not sell personal information.

5. Ads and tracking

Ads are requested as non-personalized ads. The app does not show a tracking permission prompt and does not read the advertising identifier itself. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may process ad impressions, clicks and device diagnostics. If you choose a rewarded ad, our server processes the reward confirmation signed by Google and a transaction ID that prevents duplicate rewards.

6. Retention and deletion

Your profile, learning progress, game records, friends, missions, achievements, season and arena data and notification device token are kept until you delete them. Completed match records and report or block information may remain, with identifying information minimized, for as long as needed for fair operation, abuse prevention and dispute handling. Purchase transaction records may be kept as needed for restore, duplicate-reward prevention and refund handling.

7. Your choices and rights

Delete your data yourself in the app under Settings & Account > Terms, Support & Data > Delete my game data. Deletion removes your nickname and avatar, progress, friends and notification device token, and active games are resigned. If you cannot use the app, email us below with your nickname to request deletion. You can turn off correspondence-game alerts on the Online Play screen at any time, and unblock players in Settings.

8. Children

The Service does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. A parent or guardian who believes a child’s information was processed can request deletion by emailing us or through the support page.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will state the effective date and the changes on this page and in the app.
