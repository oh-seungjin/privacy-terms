Delete your Hansu Gomoku account and data

Next Studio · 넥스트 스튜디오 · Effective October 30, 2026

Delete in the app

  1. Open Hansu Gomoku.
  2. Tap Settings & Account at the bottom.
  3. Open Terms, Support & Data.
  4. Choose Delete my game data and confirm.

If you cannot open the app

Email dhalska2@gmail.com with your nickname and the app version. We will delete the data after confirming the request.

What is deleted

Your anonymous or linked account, nickname, avatar, profile, game progress and records, coins, unlock tickets and cosmetics you own, friends and blocks, and notification device tokens. Games in progress are resigned.

What may be kept

Purchase records required by Google Play or for refunds and legal obligations, and reports kept to prevent abuse, are kept only as long as needed.
