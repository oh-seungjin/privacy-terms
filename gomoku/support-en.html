Help & Support

Effective date: October 30, 2026

1. Can I start right away?

From Home, choose Play vs AI or Learn. You can pick Freestyle, exact-five or Renju rules, play five AI levels, or play two players on one device.

2. How do online games work?

In Online Play choose a random opponent, a friend invite code, or a correspondence game with 48 hours per move. Live games give each player 15 minutes plus 5 seconds per move. The first time you go online you choose a nickname and avatar.

3. My opponent left or my connection dropped.

Reopen the app and go to Online Play to return to your game. If your opponent's clock runs out they lose on time. If a move could not be sent, the board returns to the previous position so you can play it again.

4. How do I report or block a player?

Use the ⋮ menu at the top right of a game to report, block or add the opponent as a friend. Unblock people under Settings & Account > Blocked players.

5. I do not get notifications.

Turn on Async game alerts on the Online Play screen and allow notifications in your device settings. Alerts for the game you currently have open are hidden to avoid duplicates.

6. What about coins, unlock tickets and purchases?

Coins and avatar unlock tickets are kept on the server, not on your device. Tap Restore purchases in the shop to restore. Cancel a subscription in App Store account settings > Subscriptions.

7. How do I move to another device?

Sign in with Apple or Google under Settings & Account to continue your progress on another device.

8. I want to delete my data.

Open Settings & Account > Terms, Support & Data > Delete my game data. If you cannot use the app, email us below with your nickname.

9. Something is wrong.

Email us below with the app version, device model, operating system and when it happened. Do not send passwords or sign-in tokens.
