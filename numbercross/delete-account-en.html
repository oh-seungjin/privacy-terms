Data Deletion

Effective / updated October 31, 2026

1. Scope of Number Cross deletion

Number Cross has no account and no sign-up. This page explains how to remove the data on your device and how to ask us to delete the anonymous server records (hint balance and ledger, ad reward records, game profile).

2. Delete device data

On iOS delete the app (not only offload it). On Android clear the app’s storage or uninstall. This removes your progress and settings; we cannot recover them afterwards. Deleting only the app does not delete server records.

3. Request deletion of server data

E-mail dhalska2@gmail.com with the subject “Number Cross Data Deletion”, the app version and the approximate date you last used the app. We respond within the legally required period. Deleting the server records removes your hint balance. Purchase records (transaction hash, product, time) may be kept for refund handling and accounting, but contain no personal data; ad removal on a store account can be restored from the store afterwards.
