Deep Mine Pop Help and support

If something is wrong, check the notes below or email us.

1. How to play

Drag to choose the direction the drill digs, or tap the side of the drill you want to dig toward; the drill digs and moves one block at a time. Tapping above only moves. The run ends when fuel runs out.

2. Out of fuel and Continue

When fuel runs out you can watch an ad to keep mining (once per run, optional). If the ad cannot be loaded, no fuel is granted, and you can settle the run with View result and carry on.

3. Remove Ads

'Remove Ads' is a one-time purchase that removes the interstitial ad shown when you leave the mining result screen. The optional rewarded ad you choose to watch when out of fuel remains available. If you changed devices or reinstalled, tap 'Restore purchase' with the same Google account.

4. Progress

Progress is saved automatically on your device and continues after you close the app. It does not transfer to other devices (it may be restored through Android backup).

5. Privacy choices

In regions that require it, you can change your ad consent under 'Privacy settings' at the bottom of the Remove Ads screen.

6. When you contact us

Please include your device model, Android version, app version and the steps that reproduce the problem.
