Deep Mine Pop Privacy Policy

Effective: November 24, 2026

Deep Mine Pop is a game by Next Studio. There are no accounts or logins and the developer does not store any user information on a server. Google processes information for ads and payments.

1. Information stored on your device

Mining records, your coins and crystals, drill upgrade levels, mission progress and claims, whether you bought Remove Ads, and the run in progress are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are not sent to the developer. If Android Auto Backup is on, this file may be included in your Google account backup (Google Drive) and restored on a new device. Google manages the backup and you can turn it off in device settings.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows two kinds of ads: a rewarded ad that you choose to watch when you run out of fuel, and an interstitial ad shown when you leave the mining result screen (not shown if you buy Remove Ads). To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device or other identifiers (advertising ID, app set ID), app interactions (ad impressions, taps, video views), diagnostics and performance data, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, and device and OS information, and share it with Google and its advertising partners for ad delivery, ad measurement, and fraud and abuse prevention. The developer does not see or store this data and only views aggregated statistics in the AdMob console. Data is transmitted encrypted. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Consent management (UMP)

In regions where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, some US states), Google's User Messaging Platform shows an ad consent form. There you can change your choice at any time under 'Privacy settings' at the bottom of the Remove Ads screen. The app does not request ads before consent is confirmed, and the game can be played without watching ads. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in Settings > Google > Ads.

4. In-app purchase (Google Play)

The Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play billing. Google handles your payment method and payment details and the developer does not receive them. To check that you own the purchase, the app uses the purchase information Google Play provides on your device (product ID, order ID, purchase token, Google's signature) for signature verification on the device and does not send it to a developer server.

5. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell user information. Information related to ads and payments is processed by Google and its advertising partners as described above; nothing is shared with other third parties.

6. Retention and deletion

The developer has no user information on a server, so there is nothing to retain or delete. On-device data is deleted by uninstalling the app or via Settings > Apps > Deep Mine Pop > Storage > Clear data; backups can be deleted in your Google account settings. You can view and delete ad-related data at https://myaccount.google.com .

7. Children and changes

This game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If this policy changes, the effective date on this page is updated.
