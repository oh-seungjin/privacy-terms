Deep Mine Pop Terms of Use and refunds

Effective: November 24, 2026

1. Using the game

Deep Mine Pop is a game for personal, non-commercial enjoyment. The app's code, images and sounds are the property of Next Studio. Copying, redistributing or modifying the app and manipulating ad impressions are not allowed. Game progress is stored on your device and can be lost if the device is lost or reset or the app is uninstalled.

2. Paid item

The only paid item is the one-time in-app purchase 'Remove Ads'. After you buy it, the interstitial ad shown when you leave the mining result screen is no longer displayed. The rewarded ad that you choose to watch remains available. The price is the local price shown by Google Play, and the purchase is tied to the Google account that bought it. You can restore it with 'Restore purchase' on another device of the same account or after a reinstall. A purchase whose payment is pending takes effect once it is approved.

3. Refunds and withdrawal

Purchases and refunds are handled under Google Play's payment and refund policies. You can request a refund from your Google Play purchase history within the period Google Play allows; if that period has passed or Google Play cannot refund it, email us the purchase time and order number and we will look into it. Your statutory rights of withdrawal are not limited. After a refund, the Remove Ads benefit may be revoked for that account.

4. Changes

If these terms change, the effective date on this page is updated.
