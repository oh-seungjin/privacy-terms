One new detective case every day

Daily Detective is a puzzle game. Everyone gets the same case each day: read five clues, study the suspects and name the culprit while keeping as many of your 6 points as you can.

  • A new case every day, the same for every player
  • Five clues, several suspects, a 6-point budget: a clue costs 1 point, a wrong guess costs 2
  • Hints, 2 extra points and earlier practice cases cost coins; earn coins with optional rewarded ads or buy coin packs
  • Share your result without spoilers
  • Available in 8 languages

The game is free to play. It contains optional in-app purchases (coin packs) and optional rewarded video ads. No sign-up is needed.
