Daily Detective Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Daily Detective is a mobile puzzle game provided by Next Studio. You do not sign up and you do not give us your name or email address.

1. Anonymous account

On first launch the app creates a random anonymous account (Supabase Auth, anonymous sign-in). The sign-in token is stored on your device. Everything we keep on our server is tied to that random ID only; it is not linked to your name, email address, phone number or any other account.

2. What our server stores

For each anonymous ID: your daily and practice play results (solved or not, tries used, clues used, time taken), your coin balance and coin ledger, hints and extra points bought with coins, rewarded-ad reward records, purchase records and refund records.

3. Purchases

Coin packs are consumable in-app purchases sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which process the payment. We never receive card or bank details. Coins are granted only after our server verifies the purchase with the store. We store the store, transaction ID, product ID, environment and time of each purchase, and refund records if a purchase is refunded.

4. Ads

The game shows rewarded video ads only (Google AdMob), and only if you choose to watch one to earn coins. Ad requests are non-personalized. The app does not use App Tracking Transparency and does not read the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA). On Android, the Google Play services advertising ID permission is declared because the AdMob SDK uses it. A reward record is saved on our server so each ad is rewarded once.

5. Crash reports

In release builds, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) sends crash logs, device model, operating-system version, app version and a Firebase installation identifier so we can fix crashes. Nothing is collected in development builds.

6. Sharing

The share button creates a spoiler-free result text and opens your device's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you tap it and choose where to send it.

7. What we do not collect

No analytics SDK, no location, no contacts, no photos, no microphone or camera access, no email address, no name.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep this data while the game is in use. Because accounts are anonymous, we cannot identify you by name. To ask us to delete the data tied to your device, email the support address and include your device model, OS version and the approximate dates you played, so we can locate your record. Uninstalling the app removes the anonymous sign-in key from your device.

9. Third parties

Supabase (backend hosting), Google (AdMob, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Play) and Apple (App Store). We do not sell your data.

10. Children and changes

Daily Detective is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13; it follows the store rules for the age rating it is given. If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page.
