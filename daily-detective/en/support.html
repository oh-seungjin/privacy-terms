Daily Detective Support

Help with playing, coins, purchases and ads. If your question is not answered here, email us.

1. How to play

Every day there is one new case, the same for everyone. Read the clues, study the suspects and choose the culprit. You start with (number of clues + 2) points: opening a clue costs 1 point and a wrong guess costs 2; the points you have left are your score. When the points run out the case ends.

2. Hints and extra points

A hint and 2 extra points can be bought with coins during a case. Their prices and limits are set by the server and shown in the game.

3. Practice

You can play earlier cases in Practice mode. Each practice play costs coins.

4. Coins

Coins are kept on our server and the app only shows the balance the server returns. You can earn coins by watching optional rewarded ads (there is a daily limit) or by buying a coin pack.

5. Lost coins or purchase problems

If coins did not arrive after a purchase, reopen the app with an internet connection and wait a moment. If they still do not appear, email us with your device model, OS version, the purchase date and the order or receipt number from Apple or Google.

6. Refunds

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple or Google. Request a refund through your store account. If a purchase is refunded, the related coins may be deducted.

7. Ad rewards

Coins from a rewarded ad arrive after the server confirms the ad was watched completely, which can take a few seconds. Closing the ad early gives no reward.

8. No internet

Coins, plays and purchases are handled by our server, so the game needs an internet connection to start a case, spend coins or receive rewards.
