Help & Support

2026-09-16

1. How to play

Each number specifies the number of lines around its cell; blank cells have no number constraint. All lines must form one closed loop with no branches or crossings. Tap between dots to place a line or X; tap the same mark to remove it. Long-press places X. Move mode supports zoom/pan; Fit resets the view. Undo, redo and hints are available.

2. Saved progress and purchases

Progress saves automatically on this device. Restart resets only the current puzzle. On iOS, use Restore Purchases in the Premium tab with the same Apple account. Buying, restoring and rechecking access require internet, with up to 24 hours offline after verification. If verification is interrupted, reconnect and restore again. Android and web do not offer purchasing. If no price appears, check connectivity and store product availability.

3. Report an issue

Email dhalska2@gmail.com with Slitherlink in the subject, app version, device/OS, reproduction steps and an optional redacted screenshot. Send only information needed to investigate. Never send passwords, verification codes or payment-card details. For store billing or refund requests, use the store’s support process.
