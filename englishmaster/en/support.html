English Master Help and Support

Effective November 2, 2026

If something goes wrong, check the items below and email us if it is not resolved.

1. I bought it but it is not applied

Check that you are signed in with the same account you bought with, then tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen. You need an internet connection.

2. I want to cancel

Use the Manage subscription button on the subscription screen to open the store's subscription page and cancel there. You keep Premium until the end of the paid period, and free features and your records remain.

3. I want a refund

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: reportaproblem.apple.com for Apple, your order history for Google Play. When a refund is approved Premium is revoked automatically.

4. A question or answer looks wrong

Email us the question number and what looks wrong. We check it and fix it as a new version.

5. When you write to us

Please include your device model, operating system version, app version and the steps to reproduce the problem.
