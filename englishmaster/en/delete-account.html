English Master Account and Data Deletion

Effective November 2, 2026

English Master stores the study records of a signed-in account on our server. You can have your account and data deleted as described below.

1. Request deletion

Email dhalska2@gmail.com with the sign-in you used (Apple or Google) and the app name (English Master). After verifying it is you we delete the data within 7 business days and let you know.

2. What is deleted

Your sign-in account and the server data connected to it: solved questions, wrong-answer notes, mock exams, review schedule and study goals. Purchase records may be kept for a set period, disconnected from your account, for refund handling and legal retention.

3. Cancel your subscription separately

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play before deleting.

4. Data on your device

Settings stored only on your device are removed when you delete the app.
