English Master Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

English Master is an English exam study app provided by Next Studio. To save your study records and verify your subscription you sign in with Apple or Google.

1. Information stored on our server

Your sign-in account identifier and email (if the provider shares it), display name and language settings, the questions you solved and the answers you chose, wrong-answer notes (including cause tags and favorites), mock exam attempts and resume state, review schedule, study goals, feature usage counts and the content of error reports you send. We use it only for grading, showing your records, review planning and usage limits.

2. Writing answers and Speaking recordings

The text you write, the audio you record in Speaking and the transcript made from it are stored under your account. Recordings go to a private storage folder that only you can play. The microphone is used only while you record a Speaking answer, and the transcript is only for checking what was said. We do not assess pronunciation, intonation or fluency. An unsent recording may stay on your device, is deleted after upload, and is deleted after 7 days at the latest.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are handled by the Apple App Store and Google Play; English Master never receives your card details. To verify your subscription we store the transaction number, product, expiry date and renewal, cancellation and refund status that the store reports.

4. AI feedback

Only when an AI feature such as AI wrong-answer feedback is available, the passage, question, choices and your answer (or your text or transcript) are sent to the AI provider's API (Anthropic) to generate the explanation. Nothing else is sent for this purpose and the result is a study estimate. This feature is currently switched off; the app will say so when it is switched on.

5. Crash diagnostics

If the released app crashes, the crash log, device model, operating system and app version and a random per-installation identifier are sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics. This data is not linked to your account and is used only to fix errors.

6. Ads and tracking

English Master has no ads, does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across apps.

7. Retention and deletion

Your account and records stay while the account exists. When you delete your account in the app's settings, your study records, written text, recordings and transcripts, AI records, subscription link and profile are deleted, and the sign-in account is deleted too (if you use another app with the same login, the sign-in account stays and only English Master data is deleted). Error reports may remain without your identity, and subscription event records may remain without a link to you. We have not set a separate retention period; information that a law requires us to keep is kept for that period.

8. Service providers and transfers

Supabase provides authentication, database and file storage (data region: Tokyo, Japan), Apple and Google provide sign-in and payments, Google Firebase Crashlytics provides crash diagnostics, and Anthropic processes AI feedback when it is switched on. We do not sell your information or share it with other third parties.

9. Your rights

You can review your records in the app and delete your account there. For access, correction or deletion requests write to the email below.

10. Children and changes

English Master is for general users and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If this policy changes we update the effective date on this page.
