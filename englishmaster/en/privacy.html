English Master Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

English Master is an English exam study app provided by Next Studio. To save your study records and verify your subscription you sign in with Apple or Google.

1. Information stored on our server

Your sign-in account identifier and email (if the provider shares it), the questions you solved and the answers you chose, wrong-answer notes (including cause tags and favorites), mock exam attempts, review schedule, study goals and settings, and your subscription status are stored on our server. We use them only to show your records and plan your review.

2. Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are handled by the Apple App Store and Google Play; English Master never receives your card details. To verify your subscription we store the transaction number, product, expiry date and renewal, cancellation and refund status the store reports.

3. AI wrong-answer feedback

When a Premium subscriber requests AI feedback, the passage, question, choices and the answer you chose are sent to Anthropic's API to generate an explanation. We send nothing else for this purpose. The result is a study estimate.

4. Ads and tracking

English Master has no ads, does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across apps. We do not use third-party analytics tools.

5. Retention and deletion

Your account and study records on the server are deleted when you request account deletion (see the Delete account page). Purchase records may be kept for a set period, disconnected from your account, for refund handling and legal retention.

6. Service providers

Supabase provides authentication and server storage, Apple and Google provide sign-in and payments, and Anthropic processes AI wrong-answer feedback. We do not sell your information or share it with other third parties.

7. Children and changes

English Master is for general users and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If this policy changes we update the effective date on this page.
