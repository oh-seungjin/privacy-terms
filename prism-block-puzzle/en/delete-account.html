Prism Block Puzzle Account Deletion

Effective November 14, 2026

If you used the optional Google sign-in, you can delete this game's account data as described below.

1. Delete in the app

Open Settings > Delete account and confirm. The link between your account and your purchase record is removed immediately and you are signed out.

2. What is deleted

The link between your account and your Remove Ads purchase record. Scores and progress are not stored with the account; they exist only on your device.

3. What is kept

Google keeps your Google Play purchase history. After deleting, Restore purchase brings Remove Ads back. A hash of the purchase token and its refund state remain, separate from your account, to prevent abuse.

4. If you cannot open the app

Email us at the address below with the subject 'Delete account' from the Google account you signed in with. We process it without undue delay after verifying it is you.
