Prism Block Puzzle Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Prism Block Puzzle is a block puzzle game provided by Next Studio. It works without an account and keeps game progress on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

Your score, best score, game in progress, sound, music and vibration settings, whether you have seen the tutorial, and your ad-removal purchase state are stored only on your device. Next Studio does not send this information to a server.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device information, and app usage and diagnostics data to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android settings; see https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. If you purchase ad removal, ads are no longer shown.

3. Purchases

Ad removal is processed by Google Play. Next Studio does not receive your payment details; the app only checks whether the purchase was made.

4. Crash reports

To fix bugs, the app uses Firebase Crashlytics to send anonymous crash reports, diagnostic information and a Firebase installation ID. These reports do not include your score or board state.

5. Deletion

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in device settings removes the information stored on your device, subject to system backup and restore behavior. There is no account, so there is no separate account-deletion process.

6. Children and changes

This game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated.
