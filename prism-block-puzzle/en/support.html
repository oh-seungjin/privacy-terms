Prism Block Puzzle Help & Support

Effective November 14, 2026

Review these tips or email us if you need help.

1. How to play

Drag the blocks at the bottom onto the board. A completely filled row or column disappears. Clear lines back to back for combo bonuses; when no block fits, the game ends. You can replay the tutorial from Settings.

2. Remove ads: purchase and restore

You can buy Remove Ads in Settings. If you changed devices or reinstalled, sign in with the Google account that made the purchase and tap Restore purchase in Settings.

3. Progress

Progress is saved automatically on your device and does not transfer to other devices.

4. When contacting us

Include your device model, Android version, app version and steps to reproduce the issue.
