Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

1. Operator and scope

This policy covers the iOS app "Janggi: Human-like AI & Online" (bundle ID com.osj.janggi) published by Next Studio, an individual developer in the Republic of Korea. Contact: dhalska2@gmail.com.

2. Account and cloud progress

So you can play without signing up, the app creates an anonymous account. Your progress (rating, achievements, attendance, solved puzzles, hint tickets, settings, an unfinished AI game, recent game records) is stored on the server under this account so it is not lost. Sign in with Apple is optional; Apple shares an identifier and, if you allow it, an email address (possibly a private relay address), used only to recognise your account across devices.

3. Online games and leaderboard

When you play online, your nickname, country, rating and the moves and results of your games are stored on the server and shown to opponents and on the leaderboard. You can report other players; reports (who, why, when) are kept to review rule violations. Reported nicknames may be checked by Anthropic's Claude AI; only the nickname text is sent.

4. Game records for AI training

When "Share games for AI training" is on in Settings, the moves and results of finished AI games are sent under your anonymous account ID to train a more human-like AI. No name or contact details are included, and you can turn it off at any time.

5. AI coach summary

When a Premium user asks for a coach summary, an engine-analysis summary of that game (side, result, accuracy, key moves and better moves) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write the summary. No account details or nickname are sent; the written summary is kept on the server so it can be shown again.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases (hint and review ticket packs, ad removal, theme pack, lifetime) and the Premium subscription are paid through Apple. We never receive payment details; we store only the transaction ID, product and subscription status to deliver and restore what you bought.

7. Notifications

Only if you allow notifications, we store a push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and the app language to tell you when it is your move, a game is found or a game ends. You can turn this off in the app or in iOS Settings.

8. Ads

Google AdMob shows non-personalised ads before and after games and for rewards. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not ask for the advertising identifier. AdMob may still process device information and approximate location (from IP) for ad delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention. Rewards are granted through Google's signed server-side verification.

9. Crash reports

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and error reports (device model, OS and app version, technical logs, a random installation ID) to fix problems. No payment information is included.

10. Service providers and location

Supabase (database, sign-in), Apple (Sign in with Apple, payments), Google (AdMob, Firebase notifications and crash reports) and Anthropic (coach summaries, nickname review) process data under their own privacy policies, and servers may be outside your country. This site is served by GitHub Pages and uses no analytics or ad scripts.

11. Retention and deletion

Server data is kept while your account exists. Use Settings > Delete Janggi data in the app, or email us, and this game's server data is deleted or anonymised within a reasonable time. Deleting the app removes only the data stored on the device.

12. Your rights, children, changes

Email dhalska2@gmail.com to access, correct or delete your data. The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information. Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new date.
