Help & Support

Effective date: October 4, 2026

1. I don't know the rules. Can I still start?

Open the Learn tab > Learn janggi. The 14-step tutorial lets you move the pieces yourself, from how each piece moves to check, mate and bikjang.

2. How do I choose the AI's strength?

Tap "Play the AI" and pick from 18 kyu to 9 dan. The app remembers your last side, setups and level.

3. How do bikjang and points work?

If the two generals face each other through a full move, the game is decided on remaining material (chariot 13, cannon 7, horse 5, elephant 3, guard 3, soldier 2, plus 1.5 for Han). You can make it a draw in Settings.

4. What are the online time controls?

Realtime games are 15 minutes plus byo-yomi 3 × 30 seconds. Free players can run 3 correspondence games at once; Premium has no limit.

5. How do I restore purchases?

Tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom of the shop. Cancel a subscription in App Store account settings > Subscriptions.

6. How do I move to another device?

Sign in with Apple in Settings to continue your rating, achievements, records, settings and unfinished AI game on another device.

7. Something is wrong

Email dhalska2@gmail.com with the app version, device model and what happened.
