Delete Data

Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. Delete in the app

Open Settings, tap "Delete my data", and confirm. Deletion happens immediately.

2. Request by email

If you cannot use the app, email dhalska2@gmail.com with your nickname and friend code (or purchase receipt number). We process the request after verifying it is you.

3. What is deleted

Nickname, avatar, rating, record and statistics, missions and attendance, coins and unlocks, friends and invitations, arena and season results, purchase records and push registration. This cannot be undone.

4. What remains

Your anonymous ID inside game records that other players may replay is deleted together with those records within 90 days. Payment records kept by Google and Apple follow their own policies.
