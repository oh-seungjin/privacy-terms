Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. Operator

Nyan Mahjong: Riichi 3D (the "app") is operated by Next Studio. Contact: dhalska2@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

The app does not ask for an email address, phone number or real name. When you first open it, a random anonymous account ID is created automatically. We also store: (1) the nickname and avatar you choose; (2) game data: results, placements, rating and statistics, mission and attendance progress, coins and unlocks, weekly arena and monthly season results; (3) friend code, friend list, invitations, and report/block records; (4) records of online games (for replays); (5) purchase data: the product ID and the purchase token issued by the store (we never receive card details); (6) a push token and the app language, to send notifications; (7) crash reports: device model, OS version, app version and error details when the app crashes.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data. We do not read the advertising ID or track you across apps. The app shows interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. For ad delivery and invalid-click protection AdMob may collect device information, approximate location (from IP address), app interaction and diagnostic data. Buying Remove Ads or Premium turns off interstitial ads.

4. How we use it

To provide the game (play, matching, rating, rankings, friends, replays, spectating), to confirm and deliver purchases, to send notifications, to handle reports and keep play fair (automated signals from finished ranked games), and to find and fix crashes.

5. What other players see

In online games other players see your nickname, avatar, rating, record and ranking titles. Do not put personal information in your nickname. Running quick-match games can be watched with a 3-minute delay; you can turn this off in the spectate window.

6. Service providers

Data is processed by Supabase (servers and database), Google (Firebase crash reports and push notifications, Google Play billing, AdMob ads) and Apple (App Store billing and push notifications). We do not sell your information. The ad-related data above is provided to Google AdMob to show ads. We disclose other information only when the law requires it.

7. Retention

Account data is kept until you delete it. Online game records are deleted automatically 90 days after the game. Unsent notification jobs are discarded after one day. Crash reports follow Firebase retention (up to 90 days).

8. Deletion

You can delete your data in the app under Settings > "Delete my data", or request it as described on the "Delete Data" page. Your nickname, rating, friends, purchase records and notification registration are deleted immediately. Game records that other players may replay are deleted automatically within 90 days.

9. Security

All communication uses HTTPS. Server data is access-controlled so that each account can only read and write its own data.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. If we learn that we collected a child's information, we delete it.

11. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we post the new effective date here. Contact: dhalska2@gmail.com
