Support

Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. Getting started

If you are new, learn the rules in Settings > "Tutorial". Choose the AI level in Settings.

2. Online play

Open online play from "Play" to choose quick match or a friend room. A nickname is required for online play. If four players do not gather, AI fills the empty seats and the game is unrated.

3. Restoring purchases

If a purchase is missing, tap "Restore purchases" in the shop. It restores on devices signed in with the same Google account.

4. Report and block

Tap an opponent during a game to report, block or add as a friend. Unblock in Settings > Block list.

5. Notifications

Notifications are sent for friend requests, room invitations, and arena and season results. You can turn them off in Settings.

6. Contact

Email dhalska2@gmail.com with your app version, device model and a description of the problem.
