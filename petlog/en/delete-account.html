Delete your Petlog account and data

Effective October 9, 2026

Petlog works without signing in, and data is stored on our server only if you sign in. You can delete it as follows.

1. Delete in the app (recommended)

In Petlog, choose More → Account → Delete account. All pet records and photos on our server are deleted immediately, and the sign-in account is deleted too if no other app of ours uses it. Subscriptions are not cancelled automatically, so cancel in the store separately.

2. If you cannot open the app

Email dhalska2@gmail.com with the sign-in method (Apple or Google) and the app name. After verifying it is you, we delete the data within 7 business days and let you know.

3. Data on your device

Records stored only on your device are removed when you delete the app.

4. What is deleted

Pets and their vaccine, medicine, weight, health note, vet visit and expense records, uploaded photos, family-sharing member information, and subscription status records. Payment history in the stores is managed by Apple and Google and is not deleted.
