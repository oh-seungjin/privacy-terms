Petlog Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Petlog is a pet health record app provided by Next Studio. If you do not sign in, all your records stay on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

Pet names, species, breed, birthdays and photos; vaccine, medicine, weight, health note (with photos), vet visit and expense records; reminder times; and display, language, unit and app-lock settings are stored only on your device. Face ID or fingerprint data used for app lock is handled by the operating system; the app never receives it.

2. Sign-in and information stored on our server

If you sign in with Apple or Google, your account identifier and email (if the provider shares it) are stored with our authentication service (Supabase). If you are a signed-in Pro user and turn on sync, your pets, all records and photos are stored on our server (Supabase) for device sync and family sharing. If you do not sign in, nothing is sent to a server.

3. Family sharing

When you invite family with an invite code, the invited person can view (and, depending on the role, edit) the records of the pet you shared. The member list shows emails only in a masked form (for example ab***@gmail.com).

4. Subscriptions and payments

Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Petlog never receives your card details. To confirm your subscription, the app sends the store purchase receipt (transaction ID, product, expiry date) to our server, which verifies it with Apple or Google and stores the subscription status.

5. Crash information

If the app crashes, an error report (device model, operating system version, app version, error details and a random installation ID) is sent to Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix the problem. It does not include pet records or names. Development builds do not collect it.

6. Ads and tracking

Petlog has no ads, does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across apps. We do not use third-party analytics tools.

7. Notifications, camera, photos

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; their content is not sent to a server. The camera and photo library are accessed only when you add a photo, and you can turn off the permission in device settings.

8. Retention and deletion

Records on your device are removed when you delete the app or a pet. Server data is deleted immediately, together with all records and photos, when you choose More → Account → Delete account while signed in; the sign-in account is deleted too if no other app of ours uses it. If you cannot open the app, you can request deletion by email as described on the delete-account page.

9. Service providers

Supabase provides authentication and server storage, Google (Firebase) provides crash reporting, and Apple and Google process payments. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else.

10. Children and changes

Petlog is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated.
