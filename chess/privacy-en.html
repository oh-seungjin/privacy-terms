Privacy Policy

Effective / Updated: September 29, 2026

1. Operator and scope

This policy applies to the iOS app "Chess" (bundle ID com.osj.chess), published by Next Studio (an individual developer based in South Korea). Contact: dhalska2@gmail.com. An Android version has not been released yet.

2. On-device data

Stats, achievements, the list of solved puzzles, lesson/drill progress, the daily-challenge streak, and settings (board theme, piece style, language, sound) are stored on your device by default. The coach voice narration uses only your device's built-in speech synthesis — it never records audio or sends it anywhere.

3. Accounts and online play

Most features work without signing in — the app automatically creates an anonymous account. Playing online (ranked matchmaking, friend invites) and appearing on the ranking store your chosen nickname, country, avatar, badge, and rating on our server against that anonymous account. Signing in with Apple (optional) shares an email address through Apple, used to authenticate your account.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

In-app purchases (remove ads, themes, hints, undos) and subscriptions (monthly/yearly premium) are billed through Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment card details — only whether a purchase or subscription is active is checked and stored on our server.

5. Progress sync

Only when you're signed in with Apple, your stats, achievements, solved-puzzle record, lesson/drill completion, daily-challenge record, and Puzzle Rush best score are stored on our server and merged/synced across your devices for continuity. This sync never happens while you're only playing anonymously.

6. Ads and privacy choices

Screens available for free may show Google AdMob ads. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier, device information, and an approximate (IP-based) location for ad delivery and measurement. You can decline personalized ads via iOS App Tracking Transparency, or remove ads with an in-app purchase.

7. Server retention and cross-border processing

Account, purchase, online-play, and progress data are stored on servers operated by Supabase (our database/authentication provider). These servers may be located abroad and are processed under the laws applicable there. Apple (App Store billing) and Google (AdMob) each process data under their own privacy policies.

8. Contact and website vendor

Send inquiries or deletion requests to dhalska2@gmail.com. These policy pages are statically hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.); this website itself uses no separate analytics or advertising scripts.

9. Retention and deletion

Server-side account, purchase, and progress data is kept while your account exists, and deleted or anonymized within a reasonable time after a deletion request. Deleting the app removes on-device local data, but server-side data (if you signed in) requires a separate deletion request.

10. User rights, children, and changes

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. This app is intended for users 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. This policy may change as features change; the date at the top of this page is updated when it does.
