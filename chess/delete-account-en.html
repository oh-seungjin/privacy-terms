Delete Data

Effective / Updated: September 29, 2026

1. Scope of Chess data deletion

There's no full in-app account-deletion button yet. 'Sign out' only signs you out on this device and does not delete server-side data. To delete your server account (nickname, avatar, badge, rating, purchase records, synced progress), please email us at the address below.

2. Deleting on-device data

To remove local data on your device (stats, achievements, settings, etc.), fully delete the app on iOS ('Delete App', not 'Offload App'). Reinstalling the app afterward will not restore this data.

3. Requesting deletion of account, purchase, and progress data

Email dhalska2@gmail.com with the subject 'Chess Data Deletion'. Include the email you signed in with (if you used Apple Sign-In) or other information that identifies your device/account so we can verify your request, and we'll delete the server-side data within a reasonable time. If you have an online match in progress, deletion may take a little longer so the other player's game isn't disrupted.
