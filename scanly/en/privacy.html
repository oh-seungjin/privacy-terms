PageBeam Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

PageBeam is a document and PDF scanner app provided by Next Studio. If you do not sign in, all your documents stay on your device and nothing is sent to a server.

1. Information stored on your device

Scanned photos, the PDF documents and previews you create, the searchable text read by text recognition, saved signature images, and display, language and scan settings are stored only on your device. Scanning and text recognition run on your device; photos and text are not sent anywhere.

2. Camera and photos

The camera is used only to scan documents. Importing photos uses the operating system photo picker, so the app does not access your whole photo library; only the photos you choose are passed to the app. You can turn off the permission in device settings.

3. Sign-in and information stored on our server

If you sign in with Apple (Google on Android) to use cloud backup and sync, your account identifier and email (if the provider shares it) are stored with our authentication service (Supabase). Signing in is optional and is not needed to scan, use the PDF tools, or buy a subscription.

4. Cloud backup

If you are a signed-in PRO subscriber and turn on sync, your PDF documents, preview images, signature images, document titles, folders, page counts and searchable text are stored on our server (Supabase) and synced to your other devices. Password-protected PDFs are uploaded as encrypted files, without previews or searchable text. If you turn sync off or do not sign in, your documents are not sent to a server.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. PageBeam never receives your card details. When you are not signed in, your subscription status is checked only on your device and with the store. When you are signed in, the app sends the store purchase receipt (transaction ID, product, expiry date) to our server, which verifies it with Apple or Google and stores the subscription status for cloud backup access.

6. Crash information

If the app crashes, an error report (device model, operating system version, app version, error details and a random installation ID) is sent to Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix the problem. It does not include document content, titles or photos. Development builds do not collect it.

7. Ads and tracking

PageBeam has no ads, does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across apps. We do not use third-party analytics tools.

8. Retention and deletion

Documents on your device are removed when you delete the app or a document. Server data (documents, signatures, subscription records) is deleted when you choose Settings → Account & backup → Delete account while signed in; if you used Sign in with Apple, the link to Apple is revoked too. If you cannot open the app, you can request deletion by email as described on the delete-account page.

9. Service providers

Supabase provides authentication and server storage, Google (Firebase) provides crash reporting, and Apple and Google process payments. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else.

10. Children and changes

PageBeam is for general users and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If this policy changes, we update the effective date on this page.
