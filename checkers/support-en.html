Help & Support

Effective / Updated: October 4, 2026

1. How to play

On Home choose a level and rules to play the computer, or tap Start game for two players on one device, live or async online play, friend-code games or nearby play. Learn has the rules and how to play; the tabs below lead to rankings, stats and achievements, and the shop. In game records you can replay and analyse past games and copy or import them as PDN.

2. Troubleshooting nearby play

On both devices choose nearby play under Start game; one hosts and the other joins. Wi-Fi needs the same network, and Bluetooth must be on. If they cannot connect, check in iOS Settings > Checkers that Bluetooth and Local Network are allowed, and keep the devices close.

3. Progress, purchases and sync

Progress is saved to your account automatically. Use Restore Purchases in the Shop to get your purchases back, and sign in with Apple in Settings to continue on another device.

4. Contacting us

Put "Checkers" in the email subject and tell us the app version, your device and iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. For purchase issues, the Apple receipt helps us answer faster.
