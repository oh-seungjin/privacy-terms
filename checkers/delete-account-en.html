Delete Data

Effective / Updated: October 4, 2026

1. Delete in the app

If you signed in with Apple: Settings > Account > Delete account. Your progress, ranking, nickname, purchase entitlements, coins, remaining paid hints and undos, friends and friend code, challenge history, game records synced to the server, tournament entries and push token are permanently deleted, and online games in progress are ended. App Store payments are not refunded.

2. Request by email

Without Apple sign-in, email dhalska2@gmail.com with the subject "Checkers Data Deletion" and details that identify your account (for example your nickname). We delete the server data within a reasonable time.

3. Data on the device

Data stored on the device (such as local copies of game records) is removed when you delete the app (“Delete App”, not “Offload App”). The nickname and moves sent to another device during nearby play may remain on that device and we cannot delete them.
