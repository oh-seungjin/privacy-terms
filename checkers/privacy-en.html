Privacy Policy

Effective / Updated: October 4, 2026

1. Operator and scope

This policy applies to the iOS app "Checkers" (bundle ID com.osj.checkers), published by Next Studio, an individual developer based in South Korea. Contact: dhalska2@gmail.com.

2. Account and progress

No sign-up is needed: the app automatically creates an anonymous account. Your progress (stats, achievements, solved puzzles, missions, settings and an unfinished game) is saved on our server against that account so it is not lost. Coins, hints, undos, owned skins, attendance bonuses, daily free uses and claimed achievement and mission rewards are granted, deducted and recorded by the server; the app only shows the values the server returns. A rewarded ad counts once and only after AdMob server-side verification, for which your account identifier and a one-time token are passed to our server. Signing in with Apple is optional; Apple shares an identifier and, if you allow it, an email address (which may be a private relay address), used only to recognise your account on your devices. When you sign in to an existing account, the progress of the anonymous account is merged into it.

3. Online play, rankings and spectating

When you play online, your nickname, country, avatar, rating, and the moves, result and emoji reactions of the game are stored on our server and shown to your opponent and in the rankings. Other players may watch your live online games (with your nickname shown); you can turn this off in Settings. You can report or block other players in the app; reports (the reported player, reason and time) are kept to review nicknames and conduct that break the rules.

4. Friends and tournaments

If you use friends, the app creates an 8-character friend code and stores your friend list and your challenges (game requests) on our server. Friends can see your nickname, rating and when you were last online (updated while you use the app). If you share your friend code, others can find and add you. If you join a tournament, your entry (score, games, wins, rating, rank and prize coins) is stored on our server and used for the standings.

5. Game record sync

Finished games (against the computer, online, nearby, and two players on one device; not practice) are saved on your device and also synced to our server so you can view them on another device. A record can contain the game ID, time, rules, mode, result, all moves, the starting position (for example one made in the board editor), the level, and the opponent’s nickname for online and nearby games. The server keeps your latest 20 games (200 with Premium) and the oldest are removed first. You can delete individual records in the records list.

6. Nearby play (Wi-Fi and Bluetooth)

Nearby play lets you play a device close to you directly, without internet, over the same Wi-Fi or Bluetooth LE. On Wi-Fi the room is announced and found on the local network with Bonjour (mDNS); on Bluetooth the opponent is found by advertising and scanning. While you host, your nickname is visible to nearby devices. Once connected, your nickname, avatar, rules, moves, resign and draw offers and emoji reactions go straight to the other device and are not sent to our servers. The app does not use your location. iOS asks for Bluetooth and local network access the first time; the rest of the app works if you decline, and you can change it in iOS Settings. Nearby games do not count toward rating, stats, missions or coin rewards. However, a finished game is saved on your device as a game record with the mode “nearby” and is included in the game record sync above, so it is also uploaded to our server (with the opponent’s nickname). Nearby play has no report or block function.

7. Features processed on your device

The 13 rule variants, house rules, positions made in the board editor, move notation and game analysis are calculated on your device. PDN import only reads text you paste or type, and export only copies to the clipboard; the app does not send either to a server. When you open an analysis, only a hash identifier of the game is sent to the server to count your free uses per day, not the moves themselves. If such a game is finished and kept as a record, it is included in the game record sync above.

8. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases (remove ads, theme pack, hint and undo packs) and the Premium subscription are billed by Apple. We never receive your payment details; we store the transaction ID, product and subscription status to grant and restore what you bought.

9. Notifications

Only if you turn on notifications is a push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) stored, to tell you when it is your turn or a game has ended. You can turn this off in the app or in iOS Settings.

10. Ads

Google AdMob shows non-personalized ads. Where required (for example the EEA and UK), a Google consent form (UMP) is shown before ads and you can change your choice in Settings. The app does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites and does not ask for access to the advertising identifier. AdMob may still process device information and an approximate (IP-based) location to deliver ads, limit how often they appear and prevent fraud. Ads can be removed with an in-app purchase.

11. Crash reports

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and error reports (device model, OS and app version, technical logs and a random installation ID) so we can fix problems. These reports contain no payment details.

12. Service providers and storage

Supabase (database and sign-in), Apple (Sign in with Apple and payments) and Google (AdMob ads, Firebase notifications and crash reports) process data under their own privacy policies; their servers may be located outside your country. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and use no analytics or advertising scripts.

13. Retention and deletion

Server data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account in the app or ask us by email, it is deleted or anonymized within a reasonable time. Deleting the app removes only the data stored on the device.

14. Your rights, children and changes

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data at dhalska2@gmail.com. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new date.
