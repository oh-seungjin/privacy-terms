Animal Grid Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Animal Grid is a grid puzzle app provided by Next Studio. It is intended for users aged 13 and older and works without an account.

1. Information handled on your device

Stage progress, tutorial progress, the animal collection and unlocks, and the language setting are stored only on your device.

2. Collection and sharing

Next Studio does not collect this information or send it to its own servers. The app has no account, cloud sync, or analytics tool.

3. Ads and consent

The app may show interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google and its ad partners may collect and use the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, under Google's policies. In the European Economic Area, the UK and similar regions, the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for your consent, and you can change your choice in the app where available. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in device settings.

4. Age

Animal Grid is intended for users aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Deletion

Clear the app's data or uninstall the app to remove local records, subject to system backup and restore behavior.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated.
