Animal Grid Help & Support

Review these tips or email us if you need help using the app.

1. If your progress is gone

Progress is stored only on your device, without an account. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app can remove it.

2. Ads and consent issues

If ads do not appear or you want to see the consent message again, check the privacy options in the app settings where available. You can manage the advertising ID in device settings.

3. Delete data

Clear the app's data in device settings or uninstall the app. Next Studio servers hold no user data.

4. When contacting us

Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue.
