1. Service and storage

StreamLine Pulse is a Numberlink/Flow-style puzzle where you connect two dots of the same color with a line to fill every cell on the board. Levels are procedurally generated and effectively unlimited, alongside a daily puzzle and a time trial mode. Progress is stored on your device and synced across devices via Supabase when signed in.
