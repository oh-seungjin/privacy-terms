Eliminar datos

Effective October 18, 2026

Delete from the app

Sign in, open Account, and choose Fieldwork Online Data Delete. This immediately deletes Fieldwork publishing, AI, receipt and entitlement records, then signs this app out.

Local data

The online delete action does not remove jobs, photos or backups on your device. Delete them in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall after exporting anything you need.

Shared account

The Apple/Google-backed sign-in identity is shared infrastructure and is not deleted by Fieldwork, so other Next Studio apps are not affected.

Cannot open the app

Email us from the address used to sign in with subject “Fieldwork data deletion”. We may ask you to verify account ownership. Store transaction records controlled by Apple or Google follow their policies.
