Privacy Policy

2026-09-23

1. Operator and scope

Next Studio, based in South Korea, operates Neon Block Pulse (com.osj.neonblockpulse). This policy covers the iOS/Android app and this support website. Privacy contact: dhalska2@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Your active board, personal bests for Marathon, Sprint, Ultra and Cheese Clear, and your language, haptics and control settings are stored on your device to resume play and show personal stats.

3. Accounts and the coin wallet

On launch, the app silently creates an anonymous account (no sign-in screen shown) so your coins, skins, board themes, badges and season progress can sync to our Supabase server. Signing in with Apple (iOS) or Google (Android) links the same identifier and any provided email so progress can sync across devices and you can join the global leaderboard. Signing in is optional; the coin wallet works without it.

4. Purchases

Coins are a consumable product sold through the store. Apple or Google processes payment; our server verifies the transaction identifier and product to credit coins. We do not store card details or raw receipts.

5. Advertising

We use Google AdMob and its consent SDK (UMP) to show rewarded and interstitial ads. Google may process device and ad-interaction data and IP addresses (which can suggest an approximate location) to serve ads, verify rewards, prevent fraud and produce diagnostics. We request non-personalized ads by default; change your choice in Settings under Ad privacy options.

6. Global leaderboard

If you sign in, your score, lines cleared, chosen badge and a masked version of your email appear on the public per-mode leaderboard. Don't sign in if you'd rather not appear there.

7. Providers and international transfers

We use Supabase (accounts, wallet, leaderboard), Google AdMob/UMP (ads), Google and Apple (sign-in), Gmail (support) and GitHub Pages (this website). These providers may process data outside South Korea.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you clear app storage or delete the app. Server-side coins, skins, season progress and leaderboard records can be deleted immediately from the Settings tab, or by emailing us. Transaction records required by law may be kept separately for a limited period.

9. Your rights

Email us to request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent. We may need to verify your identity to process a request.

10. Children and security

This service is not directed to children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. We use reasonable safeguards, including limiting access, but cannot guarantee absolute security.
