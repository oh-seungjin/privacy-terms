Data Deletion

2026-09-23

1. Delete your account in-app

If you're signed in, open the Settings tab and use Delete account, then follow the prompts. This removes server-side data for this game — coins, skins, season progress and leaderboard records. Your Apple/Google sign-in itself and other games' accounts are not affected.

2. Delete data from your device

Delete the app on iOS (not Offload App) or clear this app's storage from system settings on Android. Deleted local records cannot be recovered.

3. Request deletion by email

If you can't use the app, email dhalska2@gmail.com with the subject Neon Block Pulse Data Deletion. We may ask for minimal information to verify your identity, and we'll act within the period required by applicable law. Transaction records we must keep by law may be excluded.
