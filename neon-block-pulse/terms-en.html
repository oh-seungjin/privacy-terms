1. The service

These terms apply to Next Studio's Neon Block Pulse. We offer falling-block puzzle modes — Marathon, Sprint, Ultra and Cheese Clear — for free. Progress is stored on your device and, depending on sign-in, synced to our server.

2. Coins and cosmetics

Coins are earned by playing, from daily rewards, or purchased as a consumable in-app product. Coins can be spent on block skins, board themes, badges and continue tokens. Skins, themes and badges are purely cosmetic; a continue token grants one extra life on game over in place of watching a rewarded ad.

3. Season progress

Each season runs roughly 30 days. XP and unclaimed rewards do not carry over to the next season — claim rewards before the season ends or they're forfeited.
