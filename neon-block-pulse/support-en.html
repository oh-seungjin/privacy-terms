Help & Support

2026-09-23

1. How to play

Move, rotate and drop blocks with on-screen buttons or swipes to clear a full row. Marathon speeds up every 30 seconds; Sprint times you clearing 40 lines; Ultra is a 2-minute score attack; Cheese Clear has you dig through pre-stacked garbage.

2. Coins and purchases

Earn coins by playing, from the daily reward, or buy them from the Coin shop in the Settings tab. If a purchase doesn't show up, check your connection and try again — coin spends are verified server-side and reflected live.

3. Sign-in and rankings

Every feature works without signing in. To sync progress across devices or join the global leaderboard, sign in with Apple (iOS) or Google (Android) from the Settings tab.

4. Contact us

Put Neon Block Pulse in the subject line and email your app version, device/OS and reproduction steps to dhalska2@gmail.com. Don't send passwords, verification codes or payment details. For store billing or refunds, contact the relevant store's support.
